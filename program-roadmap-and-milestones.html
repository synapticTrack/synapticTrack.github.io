Program Roadmap and Milestones

synapticTrack develops reproducible beam-dynamics workflows for RAON LEBT and MEBT studies, followed by neural surrogate modeling and accelerator-control applications. The program is staged so that each modeling step is supported by validated simulation data, explicit beam-physics conventions, and reusable package APIs.

Completed Foundations

  • LEBT and MEBT TRACK lattice workspaces, notebook workflows, and deterministic production configurations are established.
  • LEBT no-offset Sobol sampling supports fixed-injection and fixed-current production datasets, with resumable HDF5 batches and validity metadata.
  • Multi-target MLP and physics-regularized MLP workflows report diagnostic, component-wise beam-state, Courant-Snyder/emittance, and covariance-validity metrics.
  • Dataset readers, training artifacts, lattice controls, and beam-state reports use versioned compatibility contracts.

Current Focus

  • Generate staged MEBT Sobol datasets with the injected beam state, MEBT lattice-control manifest, and fixed-current TRACK configuration.
  • Train and validate MEBT multi-output surrogate models using the same reproducibility, artifact, and physics-metric conventions as the LEBT workflow.
  • Maintain publication-ready diagnostic and beam-state evaluation tables without treating mixed-unit aggregate RMSE as a physical acceptance metric.

Planned Milestones

  1. Complete MEBT surrogate validation across staged sample counts and operating regions.
  2. Use validated surrogates for orbit-correction studies with bounded corrector actions and physically meaningful loss/termination conditions.
  3. Extend beam-state inference from diagnostic observations through frozen surrogates with covariance-validity checks.
  4. Compare surrogate-guided methods against high-fidelity TRACK simulations and develop higher-fidelity reconstruction methods as data and validation become available.

Documentation Policy

Reusable implementation, dataset, and physics contracts are documented under docs/. The website publishes stable user-facing documentation and excludes internal development prompts and operating instructions.
